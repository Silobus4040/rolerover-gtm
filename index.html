Step 2 · Scrape the selected groups
Tick at least one group in Step 1 to enable Start scraping.
Blank means everything: all posts in the time window and all their comments. Posts cost about $0.005 each and comments $0.0005 each, so a busy group over 30 days can be a few dollars. A career coach's ad post is not a lead, but the people replying under it can be, so comments are always read.
Step 3 · Daily watch
Every group you scrape is added here. Each day the server scrapes the last 30 hours of every group in this list and re-checks the comments on the last 7 days of posts. Delete a group to stop watching it (you can restore it).
Save your access key in Settings to load this list.
The automatic daily timer is switched on separately by the person who set this up. "Run now" costs about $0.005 for each new post.
